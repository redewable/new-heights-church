# Child Protection Statement.

**Last updated:** April 21, 2026. This statement summarizes the New Heights Church Child Protection Policy, which is in effect in every room, every service, every week. The full policy is available on request from the Church office.

**Our commitment is simple: zero tolerance for abuse of any kind.** The safety and dignity of every child who steps into this house — in Young Lions, in Youth Army, in any part of the Church — is a sacred trust. We take it with the full weight it deserves.

---

## What we do

- **Every volunteer is background-checked.** No one serves in Young Lions, Youth Army, or any role with access to minors until a clear screen is on file.
- **Two-adult rule.** No child is ever alone with a single volunteer. Every classroom, transport, and one-on-one interaction runs with two or more screened adults present.
- **Secure check-in.** At Young Lions, a tag-matching system ensures only the adult who checked a child in can pick them up.
- **Open-door culture.** Rooms stay visible — windows clear, doors open when possible, lines of sight maintained.
- **Ongoing training.** Volunteers receive regular child-safety training and sign a code of conduct annually.

## What we report

New Heights Church complies with Texas child-abuse reporting laws. If anyone on our team has reasonable cause to believe a child has been abused or neglected, the matter is reported immediately to the Texas Department of Family and Protective Services and to local law enforcement as required.

This is not optional. Pastoral confidentiality does not override mandatory reporting.

## How to raise a concern

If you observe anything that feels off — in our facility, at an off-site event, or in any context involving a New Heights staff member or volunteer — please speak up immediately.

- **In person on a Sunday:** tell a pastor, a First-Touch Team lead, or a greeter. They know how to escalate.
- **By phone:** [979-314-7585](tel:+19793147585) — ask for the Executive Administration.
- **By email:** [admin@newheightschurch.info](mailto:admin@newheightschurch.info)

Concerns involving a minor's safety are always taken seriously and handled by our senior pastoral team. Reports are kept confidential to the maximum extent allowed by law.

## Emergency

If a child is in immediate danger, **call 911 first**, then notify the Church.
