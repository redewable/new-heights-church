# What we believe.

Everything at New Heights Church roots back to the Word of God. Below are the Church's own statements of faith — the doctrinal ground of the house — followed by the things this house says out loud in this season, and the three pillars we walk them out on.

## Our purpose

**To love people and point them to Christ.**

**You.** God puts a premium on people. So do we. You and your family are important to us and we look forward to serving you soon!

**Us.** New Heights has a clear vision: to love people and point them to Christ. Join us and help reach the community of Bryan/College Station!
