# Site-wide audit — 2026-09-07

Scope: every route (34) at three viewports (390 / 820 / 1440), plus a code-level
pass over tokens, components, copy, and canonical facts. Screenshots were
captured with Playwright before and after; an automated probe checked
horizontal overflow, heading structure, sub-11px text, and console errors on
every page.

Everything below is fixed unless marked **Open**.

---

## 1. Root causes (site-wide)

### 1.1 Unlayered element CSS hid text everywhere — **fixed**

`app/globals.css` set `h1…h5 { color }`, `a { color: inherit }` and the heading
size clamps _outside_ any CSS layer. Tailwind v4 puts its utilities in the
`utilities` layer, and unlayered rules beat layered ones regardless of
specificity. Consequences, confirmed in the before-screenshots:

- Every hero `h1` on an ink background (Events, Sermons, Watch, Podcasts,
  Resources, Leadership, Young Lions, Grow, I'm New, About, Visit, and the
  first line of the Home, Give, and Rise Up heroes) rendered ink-on-ink.
- Every **primary `Button` that was a link** — the header "Give", "Plan your
  visit", "What to expect", the 404 "Home", the active filter chips on Events
  and Sermons — rendered as an empty dark box.
- No link ever showed its hover color.
- The "Also on the calendar" label rendered as a full-size serif `h3`.

Fix: element defaults now live in `@layer base`, house utilities in
`@layer components`. Documented in `docs/BRAND.md` §4.6 and `CLAUDE.md`.

### 1.2 Eyebrow labels rendered lowercase — **fixed**

139 labels used `.u-smallcaps text-[11px] font-semibold` (or `10px`). The class
relies on the `smcp` OpenType feature, which Inter doesn't ship, so every label
rendered as plain lowercase 10–11px text. Replaced with one utility,
`.u-eyebrow` (Inter, 12px, 600, uppercase, +0.16em), across 49 files. Zero
arbitrary `text-[Npx]` values remain. `.u-smallcaps` is kept for true Fraunces
small caps only.

### 1.3 Altar-glow bled past the viewport — **fixed**

`.motif-altar-glow::before` used `inset: -10%`, giving I'm New, Rise Up and
Build, and Podcasts a horizontal scrollbar at every width (up to +144px on
desktop). The wash is now clipped to its box.

### 1.4 The logo had four bars — **fixed**

The Church's mark is three ascending bars, each rising and widening to the
right. The site rendered four equal-width bars everywhere. `AscendingBars` is
now inline SVG traced from the logo; the three bars are the three pillars, and
`tone="pillars"` paints them scarlet / purple / blue. A unit test enforces the
count.

## 3. Structure and accessibility

- **Duplicate `h1`** on `/grow/baptism` and `/grow/volunteer` (page hero +
  form shell). `FormShell` takes `as="h2"` on pages that already have a hero.
- **404 page** had no header or footer. It now composes both.
- **Social links** were the literal strings "FB IG YT". Now proper icons with
  40px targets and descriptive labels.
- **Touch targets:** pillar chips and sort pills are 36px tall.
- **Favicon was the create-next-app default** (the Next.js logo). Replaced
  with the three-bar mark (`app/icon.svg`) plus a generated Apple touch icon.
- **Open Graph image pointed at a file that didn't exist** (`/og/default.png`),
  so every social share had a broken card. `app/opengraph-image.tsx` now
  generates the default card at build (ink, the mark, name in Fraunces,
  tagline in gold, service times).
- **Sermon poster chips** clipped the longer pillar names on small thumbnails.
  Replaced with a pillar-colored spine on the thumbnail's left edge, label
  kept for screen readers.
- **`ConsentProvider`** called `setState` synchronously in an effect (lint
  error, blocked `pnpm verify`). Rewritten on `useSyncExternalStore` with a
  cached snapshot; also syncs across tabs and survives blocked storage.
- Unused `AscendingBars` import on the event detail page — now used.

## 9. Second pass — tone and design elevation

The first pass fixed what was broken. The Church's review of it was blunt: the
hero colors were "strange", the effect "amateur", and several details were
wrong in kind, not degree. This pass addresses the substance.

### 9.1 Tone and honor — **fixed**

- **The office is honored.** Copy now reads _Apostle Brian Hallam_ and
  _Pastor Crystal Hallam_ everywhere, never a first name alone. The letter is
  signed with full titles. The leadership page opens on 1 Timothy 5:17.
- **Removed every line that made leadership sound casually available** —
  "stay after every service", "they'll walk you over", "on the floor after
  service", "they are accessible". Pastoral care, prayer, and introductions
  now route through the First Touch Team and the Connect Card.
- **"The Book" → the Word of God.** The Beliefs page now uses the Church's own
  statement: "The Holy Bible, and only the Bible, is the authoritative Word of
  God," and the Cross of Calvary / sufficiency-of-the-Blood language.
- **Flippant lines cut:** "the coffee is free", "pass the basket", "drop it in
  the basket", "nobody gets a second look", "five minutes or five hours". The
  offering is "received as an act of worship"; guests are guests.
- **Family, not dress code.** Scrubs stay; the point is _we are family_.
- **A move of God.** New home band: "This is a move of God. … to stay on fire,
  you must have oil. The oil comes at a cost." (Matthew 25:1–13, Leviticus
  6:13), set against the Apostle at the altar.
- **No fabricated fruit.** The invented "Marcus T." testimony is no longer
  rendered. The placeholder sermon library (with joke YouTube IDs) is replaced
  by the Church's six most recent real services pulled from the New Heights
  channel.
- Pillar full names now follow Brian Hallam Ministries' own wording:
  _Laboring for the Endtime Harvest of Souls_ · _Preparing the Bride of Christ
  for the Return of the Lord_ · _A Habitation of God — Carriers of Revival_.

### 9.2 Design — **fixed**

- **Real photography.** Four Church-owned photos (from brianhallam.com):
  Apostle Hallam at the pulpit (home hero, leadership hero), at the altar
  ("move of God" band, Apostle's card), the pastors' portrait (letter, I'm New,
  About hero, Pastor Crystal's card). Registered in `lib/constants/media.ts`;
  hosts allow-listed. No stock.
- **The muddy radial "washes" are gone** from all 23 ink sections. Ink
  sections use one quiet directional gradient; photo heroes use a proper scrim
  (`.u-scrim-photo`) that keeps the left two-thirds solid for copy.
- **Header:** taller, larger mark, gold active-route underline on the nav, and
  two decisions on the right — _Watch live_ and a gold _Give_.
- **Hero:** photo-led, staged entrance, "Under the apostolic leadership of…"
- **Featured event:** the Activated poster and speakers get a full section
  directly under the hero (data-driven; disappears when nothing is featured).
- **Pillar spread:** the pale Roman numerals are replaced by the mark itself,
  with each row's bar lit in its pillar color and the other two ghosted — the
  guest learns the logo and the three things together.
- **Portraits** carry a single offset gold hairline (`.u-frame-gold`).
- **Shared `PageHero`** for inner pages so heroes stay consistent.

### 9.3 Still open

- **Sermon tags.** The six real services carry no pillar / scripture / topic
  tags (we won't invent them). Staff tag in admin, or the ingest fills them.
- The YouTube channel ID (`UCKRwjr7sT9avbEUDsY2BLiA`) is recorded in
