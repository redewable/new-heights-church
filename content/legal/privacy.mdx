# Privacy Policy.

**Last updated:** April 21, 2026. This policy is pending review by New Heights Church legal counsel; material changes will be reflected here with a new last-updated date.

New Heights Church ("we," "us," "the Church") is committed to handling personal information with care. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. If any detail below raises a question, email [admin@newheightschurch.info](mailto:admin@newheightschurch.info).

---

## What we collect

We only collect what we need to pastor you well.

- **Contact information you give us** — names, email addresses, phone numbers, and mailing addresses submitted through our Connect Card, prayer request form, baptism interest form, baby dedication form, volunteer application, or newsletter subscription.
- **Decisions at the altar** — when you record a salvation or rededication decision on our site, we capture your first name, decision type, and (optionally) email or phone so a pastor can follow up.
- **Pledges** — for capital campaigns like Rise Up and Build, we record your pledge amount, frequency, and contact details. We never collect your payment information on this site.
- **Technical context** — your IP address (hashed, never stored raw), browser user-agent, and the pages you visited while on our site.
- **Analytics** — with your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel to understand how visitors use the site. See **Analytics + consent** below.

## What we don't collect

- **Payment card details.** Online giving runs through Pushpay; crypto through Engiven; stock/securities through Edward Jones. Their systems process the transactions — your card number never touches this site.
- **Browsing data across other sites** unless you explicitly consent to third-party analytics cookies.
- **Data about visitors under 13.** We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## How we use what we collect

- To respond to prayer requests, connect-card submissions, and decisions with pastoral follow-up.
- To schedule baptisms, baby dedications, and volunteer placements.
- To send transactional emails (confirmations, Bible packets, year-end giving statements).
- To send newsletter updates, if you opted in.
- To track progress on named campaigns (Rise Up and Build, Giving Towards Growth) in aggregate — never publicly tied to individual names or amounts.
- To comply with legal obligations (tax receipts, child-protection reporting, subpoenas).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Ever.

## Who we share it with (and who we don't)

A short list, each with a specific reason:

- **Planning Center** — for people records, life groups, event registrations. They are bound by a Data Processing Agreement and we never send them payment information.
- **Mailchimp** (when active) — for newsletter delivery. You can unsubscribe from every email.
- **Resend** — for transactional email delivery.
- **Supabase** — our hosted database provider. All records live on their infrastructure under a DPA.
- **Pushpay, Engiven, Edward Jones** — each handles a different giving channel. Your gift details live in their systems; we see summary totals only.
- **Cloudflare** — for DNS and bot defense (Turnstile). They see request metadata.
- **Vercel** — for hosting. They see request metadata.
- **Law enforcement + government agencies** when compelled by a valid legal order, or when reporting a child-protection concern is required by Texas law.

We do **not** share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for marketing purposes.

## Analytics + consent

When you first visit this site we show a consent banner. You can accept analytics cookies or choose "only essential" — your selection is stored locally and we respect it on every page. Analytics tools are not loaded until consent is given.

You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's site data or emailing us.

## How long we keep your information

- **Connect cards, prayer requests, decisions, baby-dedication registrations, baptism interest** — retained indefinitely unless you ask us to delete them. Pastoral follow-up sometimes spans years.
- **Newsletter subscribers** — retained while subscribed; deleted within 30 days of unsubscription.
- **Giving records** — retained at least seven years for tax-compliance purposes, per IRS guidance.
- **Technical logs + analytics** — retained 14 months, then aggregated or deleted.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- **Access** the information we hold about you.
- **Correct** anything inaccurate.
- **Delete** your personal information (subject to retention obligations above).
- **Opt out** of marketing emails — every newsletter has a one-click unsubscribe.

Email [admin@newheightschurch.info](mailto:admin@newheightschurch.info) with any request. We respond within 30 days.

## Security

- All transmission is encrypted (HTTPS / TLS).
- Our database enforces row-level security; only authenticated staff can read submissions.
- Payment processing is handled by PCI-compliant providers; our site is never in the cardholder-data environment.
- Staff access to sensitive data requires approved email addresses and follows a least-privilege model.

No system is perfect. If we discover a material breach, we will notify affected individuals as required by applicable law.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices evolve. Material changes (new data categories, new third parties, retention-period changes) will be posted here with a refreshed **Last updated** date at the top.
